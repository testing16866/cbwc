Dr. Bongato graduated from Texas Tech University Foster School of Medicine in El Paso, Texas and completed her residency at HCA Florida Healthcare/USF Morsani College of Medicine in Brandon, Florida. Dr. Romulo Corrada is a distinguished board-certified obstetrician and gynecologist whose commitment to excellence in patient care is truly unparalleled. Graduating with an M.D. from the University of Puerto Rico School of Medicine and completing his residency at the University Hospital of Puerto Rico, Dr. Corrada brings a wealth of knowledge and experience to his practice. As a respected member of the Texas Medical Association, the American Medical Association, and the American Association of Gynecologic Laparoscopic Surgeons, he consistently stays at the forefront of advancements in his field. Beyond his professional achievements, Dr. Corrada is a dedicated family man who balances his demanding career with a passion for golf and salsa dancing. His holistic approach to life and medicine ensures that his patients receive not only top-tier medical care but also compassionate, personalized attention.
Dr. Corrada’s expertise in obstetrics and gynecology extends beyond traditional medical treatments. He believes in educating his patients on preventative care and healthy lifestyle choices to promote overall wellness. In addition to providing routine exams and screenings, he also offers a range of specialized services such as hormone replacement therapy, prenatal education classes, and minimally invasive surgical procedures.
As an advocate for women’s health, Dr. Corrada is actively involved in community outreach programs and initiatives aimed at promoting access to healthcare for underserved populations. He has also been recognized for his philanthropic efforts, including pro bono work and fundraising for various charitable organizations.
In today’s rapidly changing healthcare landscape, Dr. Corrada remains committed to staying updated on the latest technologies and treatments to provide his patients with the best possible care. He is dedicated to fostering strong doctor-patient relationships built on trust, open communication, and collaboration.
