Urogynecology

Our specialized physicians have extensive knowledge regarding evaluation and treatment of conditions that affect the female pelvic organs, and the muscles and connective tissue that support the organs. Some of the common problems treated include urinary incontinence or leakage, pelvic organ prolapse (dropping of the vagina, uterus, cystocele, rectocele), and overactive bladder.
