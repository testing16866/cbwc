What to expect from your first visit

A gynecologist is a doctor who specializes in women’s reproductive health. Obstetricians care for women during their pregnancy and just after the baby is born. They also deliver babies. An ob-gyn is trained to do all of these things.

Our providers deal with some of the most important health issues in your life, including birth control, childbirth, and menopause. We also screen for cancer, treat infections, and perform surgery for pelvic organ or urinary tract problems.

On your first visit expect to be at the office for at least an hour for initializing of the electronic medical record, health history, examination, possible testing and care plan. You will need your insurance cards, picture ID and a list of all medications including, over the counter vitamins and herbs.

Filling out the medical history form in advance will help with the initial documentation of your electronic medical record. Our professional and caring staff will be asking more medical questions that will be necessary for your ongoing care and treatment.
