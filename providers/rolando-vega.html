Rolando E. Vega

MD / FACOG
Obstetrics / Gynecology & Infertility

(361) 993-6000 | ext 7209

MD @ San Juan Puerto Rico. Fellow of American College of OBGYN
Facebook
Dr. Rolando E. Vega stands as a distinguished figure in the field of obstetrics and gynecology, holding an MD from San Juan, Puerto Rico, and serving as a Fellow of the American College of OBGYN. Dr. Vega has made significant contributions to both clinical practice and community health. Since moving to Corpus Christi in 2008, he has become a cherished part of the local medical community, despite his accent hinting at his Puerto Rican roots. Passionate about his work, Dr. Vega particularly treasures his role in deliveries, stating, “My favorite part of my career is the deliveries because I love being part of the happy event that affects the whole family.”
