Surgical Procedures Provided by CBWC

In the evolving world of medical technology, Robotic Surgery stands at the forefront, offering unparalleled precision, minimal invasiveness, and improved patient outcomes. Our state-of-the-art robotic systems are designed to assist surgeons in performing complex procedures with exceptional accuracy. Let’s delve into the key features and benefits that make Robotic Surgery an innovative solution for women’s health.
  • Hysterectomy

A hysterectomy is a surgical procedure to remove the womb (uterus). Our robotic-assisted hysterectomy procedure is a groundbreaking advancement in gynecological surgery. By utilizing robotic technology, surgeons can perform this essential procedure with enhanced precision and control. The benefits include:

  • Minimally Invasive:
    Small incisions mean less scarring and quicker recovery times.
  • Shorter Hospital Stay:
    Patients typically experience faster recovery and shorter hospital stays compared to traditional methods.

Risks & Considerations Related to Hysterectomy:

  • Injury to the ureters (ureters drain urine from the kidney into the bladder)
  • Vaginal cuff problem (scar tissue, infection, bacterial skin infection, pooling/clotting of blood, incision opens or separates)
  • Injury to bladder (organ that holds urine)
  • Bowel injury, vaginal shortening, problems urinating (cannot empty bladder, urgent or frequent need to urinate, leaking urine)
  • Myomectomy (Fibroid Removal)

Myomectomy is surgery to remove uterine fibroids. Uterine fibroids are non-cancerous growths of tissue (almost always noncancerous) that develop in the uterus. Robotic Surgery is revolutionizing the removal of uterine fibroids. With our advanced systems, surgeons can remove fibroids while preserving the uterus, which is particularly important for women who wish to maintain fertility. Highlights include:

  • Targeted Fibroid Removal:
    Enhanced visualization and precision allow for the targeted removal of fibroids while preserving healthy tissue.
  • Reduced Blood Loss:
    The precision of robotic instruments minimizes blood loss during surgery.
  • Faster Recovery:
    Smaller incisions and minimally invasive techniques contribute to a quicker return to normal activities.
  • Tubal Ligations
It’s also known as having your tubes tied or tubal sterilization. During this surgery, the fallopian tubes most often are cut and tied to prevent pregnancy for the rest of your life. Tubal ligation prevents an egg from moving from the ovaries and down to the uterus through the fallopian tubes.
  • Ovarian Cysts

Ovarian cysts are common growths that develop on or inside your ovaries.

The da Vinci system can be utilized for robotic Ovarian Cystectomy (removal of an ovarian cyst) or robotic Oophorectomy (removal of an ovary).

  • Ectopic Pregnancy
An ectopic pregnancy occurs when a fertilized egg grows outside of the uterus. Almost all ectopic pregnancies—more than 90%—occur in a fallopian tube. Salpingostomy and Salpingectomy are surgery procedures to treat Ectopic Pregnancy. In a salpingostomy, the ectopic pregnancy is removed and the tube left to heal on its own. In a salpingectomy, the ectopic pregnancy and the tube are both removed. Which procedure you have depends on the amount of bleeding and damage and whether the tube has ruptured.
  • LEEP
A LEEP procedure allows your provider to remove abnormal tissue on your cervix and test it for cancer. It’s used to diagnose and treat cervical dysplasia.
  • Endometrial Ablation
For women experiencing excessive menstrual bleeding, we offer Novasure uterine ablation—a safe and effective treatment to reduce or eliminate heavy bleeding. This minimally invasive procedure can significantly improve your quality of life.
  • Endometriosis Treatment

Endometriosis can be a debilitating condition, but Robotic Surgery offers a cutting-edge approach to treatment. By using robotic assistance, surgeons can meticulously excise endometrial tissue with greater accuracy. Key benefits are:

  • Comprehensive Removal:
    Advanced imaging and precision tools enable thorough removal of endometrial tissue, potentially reducing recurrence.
  • Less Pain:
    Minimally invasive techniques result in less post-operative pain and discomfort.
  • Quicker Recovery:
    Patients benefit from shorter recovery periods and faster resumption of daily activities.
  • Hysteroscopy
A hysteroscopy is a procedure used to examine the inside of the womb (uterus) in order to diagnose conditions. At CBWC our Doctors utilize Hysteroscopy to help diagnose problems such as heavy periods, unusual vaginal bleeding, postmenopausal bleeding, pelvic pain, repeated miscarriages or difficulty getting pregnant. This insight allows us to provide better treatment options.
  • Polypectomy
Polypectomy is a procedure used to remove polyps. Polyps are small fingerlike growths that may form on the cervix (cervical polyps) or the lining of the uterus (endometrial or uterine polyps). In most cases these polyps are benign, or noncancerous.
  • Urinary incontinence surgery
Surgery may provide a long-term solution for women with stress urinary incontinence when other treatments don’t work. Our Doctors at CBWC have years of experience in the treatment of Urinary incontinence.

Why Choose Robotic Surgery?

Robotic Surgery represents a significant leap forward in surgical innovation. Here’s why it stands out:
  • Enhanced Visualization
Surgeons benefit from a 3D high-definition view, providing a clearer and more detailed perspective of the surgical area.
  • Greater Range of Motion
Robotic instruments mimic the surgeon’s hand movements with greater range and precision, leading to better outcomes.
  • Patient-Centered Care
Reduced pain, minimal scarring, and faster recovery times mean a better overall experience for patients.
  • Transforming Women’s Health

Whether it’s a hysterectomy, myomectomy, or endometriosis treatment, our robotic systems empower surgeons to deliver the best possible care with confidence and precision.
